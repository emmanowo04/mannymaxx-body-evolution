Weekly Plan — ~1,960 kcal · 170g protein

Flexible, not strict keto. High protein every meal, carbs around gym sessions only, structured evenings.

🌅 Breakfast — before cycling or at desk
High Protein Start
2-3 scrambled eggs + 100ml egg whites + sardines or tuna + spinach. 8 minutes. Cook in butter with smoked paprika.
~400 kcal50g protein5g carbs
☀️ Lunch — Gym Days Only
Chicken + Rice + Veg
Butcher chicken + 150g basmati rice + broccoli. Eat within 90 mins of finishing your session. This is your carb window.
~600 kcal55g protein60g carbs
☀️ Lunch — Rest Days
Protein + Veg, No Rice
Chicken thighs + broccoli + half avocado. Skip the rice. Pure on Cheapside or butcher on Bow Lane.
~450 kcal50g protein8g carbs
🍎 4PM — Danger Window
Apple + Protein
One apple + 2 boiled eggs or a handful of almonds. Replaces Monster Munch and custard creams. The protein stops the sugar crash and second craving.
~200 kcal14g protein15g carbs
🌙 Dinner — 7-8PM from prep
Prepped Protein + Veg
Chicken thighs or beef mince reheated 5 mins + broccoli or spinach. Vary seasoning daily: Mon paprika/cumin · Tue coconut aminos · Wed jerk · Thu Italian · Fri butter/garlic.
~500 kcal50g protein10g carbs
📦 Sunday — 1 Hour Prep
Batch Cook Everything
1kg chicken thighs in oven 200°C 35 mins. Brown 500g beef mince in batches. Boil 8 eggs. Wash broccoli. Week sorted.
